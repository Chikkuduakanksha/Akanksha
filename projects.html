Client: American International Group, Inc, (AIG)

About AIG:

  • American International Group, Inc. (AIG) is a global leader in insurance and financial services, founded in 1919 and headquartered in New York City. With operations in more than 80 countries and jurisdictions, AIG offers a comprehensive range of insurance products, including:
  • 1. General Insurance: Commercial, personal property, casualty, and liability coverage.
  • 2. Life & Retirement: Life insurance, retirement planning, and pension solutions.
  • 3. Reinsurance and Risk Solutions: Strategic services to help businesses manage risk more effectively.
  • AIG is committed to helping clients prepare for the future, recover from loss, and grow with confidence. The company is recognized for its strong underwriting capabilities, digital innovation, and data-driven decision-making.
  • As one of the most respected names in the insurance industry, AIG supports a diverse portfolio of clients—from individuals and small businesses to Fortune 500 corporations. Its focus on operational excellence, compliance, and regulatory standards ensures world-class service and trust.
  • AIG continues to invest in advanced data analytics, digital transformation, and cloud technologies to streamline operations and improve customer experiences. With a global workforce and a diverse client base, AIG emphasizes integrity, accountability, and innovation in delivering risk and insurance solutions. In recent years, AIG has focused on:
  • 1. Modernizing legacy systems through scalable, cloud-native architectures.
  • 2. Enhancing data quality and governance to support accurate underwriting and claims analysis.
  • 3. Driving automation across data pipelines and reporting processes for faster, insight-driven decisions.
  • 4. Strengthening cybersecurity and compliance in highly regulated environments.
  • AIG’s technology teams collaborate across functions to build secure, resilient, and customer-centric platforms, making it a dynamic environment for professionals in data engineering, analytics, and application development.
